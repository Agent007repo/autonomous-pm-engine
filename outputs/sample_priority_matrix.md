# Sample Feature Priority Matrix

| Feature | Reach | Impact | Confidence | Effort | Priority Rationale |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Feedback ingestion | 9 | 8 | 8 | 5 | Foundational capability required for all downstream analysis. |
| Pain-point clustering | 8 | 9 | 7 | 6 | High product value because it converts raw feedback into decision-ready themes. |
| PRD generation | 7 | 9 | 7 | 5 | Directly supports PM workflow and creates visible output artifacts. |
| Engineering critique loop | 6 | 8 | 6 | 6 | Improves quality and makes generated PRDs more useful to technical teams. |
| Dashboard UI | 5 | 6 | 5 | 8 | Useful but should follow after reliable pipeline outputs. |

## Recommendation

Prioritize ingestion, clustering, and PRD generation first. Add critique and dashboard layers once the core synthesis pipeline is reliable and measurable.
